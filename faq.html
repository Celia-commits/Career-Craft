Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for job seekers and recruitment agencies. Can’t find yours? Ask us.

For Job Seekers

What exactly is ATS optimisation?

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are bots that scan your CV for keywords before a human sees it. We format your CV and rewrite content so bots rank you higher, ensuring you reach the recruiter’s desk.

I’m switching industries entirely. Won’t I look underqualified?

Not with us. We specialise in identifying transferable skills — taking your past wins and reframing them to match your new target industry. We speak recruiter, not just job titles.

Do you write CVs for international markets (UK, USA, Middle East)?

Yes. CV vs. Resume, spelling, date formats, photo rules — we tailor everything to the country you’re applying in. Just tell us where you’re targeting.

Is my personal information and career history safe with you?

Absolutely. We’re POPIA compliant. Your documents are used only for crafting, never shared or sold. We delete files 30 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them.

For Recruitment Agencies

Will candidates or clients know we outsourced to you?

No. Everything is white-label. We use your email signature and your tone. If you provide a template or logo during setup, we’ll match it exactly. No template = we return clean, text-only documents. You look efficient — we stay invisible.

How fast is turnaround for CV reformatting?

24-48 hours for standard batches. Urgent same-day slots available if you flag it. Reference checks: 2-3 working days depending on referees responding.

What does a reference check report look like?

We send structured email notes within 48hrs covering: dates verified, role confirmed, reason for leaving, rehire status, and any direct quotes from the referee. Simple, factual, and ready to forward to your client. No lengthy PDFs.

What about data/POPIA?

We sign mutual NDAs before starting. I’m POPIA compliant — candidate data is stored securely, used only for your brief, and deleted after 30 days. You can audit this anytime.

How does pricing work for agencies?

Volume-based, not per-hour. The more CVs/references you send monthly, the lower your per-unit cost. No retainers, no minimums. Request rates via the agency inquiry form.

Still have questions?

Job seeker or agency — we reply within 24 hours.
