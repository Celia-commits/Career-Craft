About Us

Our Mission

Your past doesn't have to dictate your future. Career Craft was founded to bridge the gap between outdated CVs and your next big move — whether you're a job seeker or a recruitment agency drowning in admin.

Why Career Craft?

75% of CVs get filtered out by automated systems (ATS). A great candidate gets lost. We translate non-traditional backgrounds into the specific language recruiters search for.

For job seekers: We craft narratives that prove you belong in the room, pivot or promotion.

For agencies: We handle the time-consuming admin — CV reformatting and reference checks — so your consultants stay client-facing and close more deals.

How We Work

1. The Discovery

We analyse your background or your candidates' raw CVs to find the unique "edge". For agencies: we learn your template + tone once.

2. The Craft

Our experts rewrite from scratch, optimising for both ATS and human recruiters. White-label for agencies — your branding, our work.

3. The Launch

You receive polished, interview-ready documents in 24-48hrs. Job seekers apply. Agencies place candidates faster.
