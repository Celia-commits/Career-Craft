Common Questions

“Will candidates know we outsourced?”
No. Everything is white-label. We use your email signature and your tone. If you provide a template or logo during setup, we’ll match it exactly. No template = we return clean, text-only documents. You look efficient — we stay invisible.

“What about data/POPIA?”
We sign mutual NDAs before starting. I’m POPIA compliant — candidate data is stored securely, used only for your brief, and deleted after 30 days. You can audit this anytime.

“What does a reference check report look like?”
We send structured email notes within 48hrs covering: dates verified, role confirmed, reason for leaving, rehire status, and any direct quotes from the referee. Simple, factual, and ready to forward to your client. No lengthy PDFs.

“How much does it cost?”
Volume-based. The more CVs/refs you send, the lower the per-unit cost. Small agencies welcome. Get a quote — no obligation.
